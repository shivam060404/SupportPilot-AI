# New Hire IT Setup — Day 1 Checklist

Welcome! Follow this checklist to get your workstation fully set up on Day 1.

## Step 1: Receive Your Equipment
- Your laptop will be pre-configured with your company account.
- If you did not receive equipment, contact HR or your manager immediately.

## Step 2: First Login
1. Power on the laptop. Enter the temporary password provided by IT.
2. You will be prompted to set a new password: `https://password.company.com`.
   - Requirements: 14+ characters, uppercase, lowercase, number, symbol.
3. Set up Microsoft Authenticator on your mobile phone:
   - Install "Microsoft Authenticator" from the App Store / Google Play.
   - Follow the MFA setup prompts that appear after your first login.

## Step 3: Connect to the Network
### Office (on-site)
- Connect to the Wi-Fi network `Corp-WiFi` using your company email and new password.
- Ethernet is available at your desk — preferred for stable connectivity.

### Remote / Work from Home
- Install the VPN client: download **Cisco AnyConnect** from `https://it-portal.company.com/vpn`.
- Connect to `vpn.company.com` using your company email and password + MFA.
- VPN is required for access to all internal systems when working remotely.

## Step 4: Set Up Email
1. Open Outlook. If it doesn't launch automatically, download Microsoft 365 from the IT portal.
2. Your email address is `firstname.lastname@company.com`.
3. Follow the Outlook Setup Guide for step-by-step configuration instructions.

## Step 5: Access Key Systems
These systems are accessible after your manager grants role-based access:
- **HR Portal**: `https://hr.company.com`
- **IT Portal**: `https://it-portal.company.com`
- **Document Storage**: OneDrive / SharePoint (synced via the OneDrive desktop app)
- **Communication**: Microsoft Teams (installed by default)
- **Ticketing**: `https://it-portal.company.com/tickets`

## Step 6: Install Required Software
Standard software is pre-installed. For additional tools:
1. Visit the **Software Center** (Windows: Start menu → Software Center).
2. Search for the required application and install.
3. If the application is not listed, raise an IT ticket with a business justification.

## Common New Hire Issues

### Cannot Log In
- Verify you are using `firstname.lastname@company.com` (not a personal email).
- Ensure the temporary password hasn't expired (valid for 24 hours).
- Call IT Helpdesk: ext. 5555.

### No Access to a System After Manager Grants Permission
- Access changes can take up to 1 hour to propagate.
- Log out and back in to refresh your access tokens.
- If still blocked after 2 hours, raise an IT ticket with your manager's confirmation.

### Software Center Is Empty or Not Loading
- Ensure you're connected to Corp-WiFi or VPN.
- Allow up to 4 hours for policies to sync on a new device.
