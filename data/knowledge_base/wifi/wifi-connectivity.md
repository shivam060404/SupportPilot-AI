# Office Wi-Fi

To connect to the corporate Wi-Fi network `CorpNet`:

1. Select `CorpNet` from the list of available Wi-Fi networks on your device.
2. When prompted, enter your standard company email and password.
3. If asked to accept a certificate, verify that it is issued to `radius.company.com` and accept it.
4. For guests, please use the `CorpGuest` network and sign in through the captive portal.

If you experience slow speeds or connection drops, try "forgetting" the network in your Wi-Fi settings and reconnecting.
