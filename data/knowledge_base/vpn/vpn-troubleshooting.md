# VPN Troubleshooting

If your VPN keeps disconnecting or failing to connect, please follow these steps:

1. **Check your internet connection**: Ensure you have a stable Wi-Fi or Ethernet connection.
2. **Restart the VPN client**: Quit the Cisco AnyConnect or GlobalProtect client entirely and reopen it.
3. **Verify credentials**: Ensure you are using your current company password and have approved the Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) prompt on your phone.
4. **Change the gateway**: Try selecting a different region or gateway from the dropdown list.
5. **Reinstall**: If the issue persists, uninstall the VPN client and download the latest version from the IT Portal.

If none of these steps resolve the issue, please raise an IT ticket.
