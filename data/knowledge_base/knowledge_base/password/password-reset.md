# Password Reset

If you have forgotten your password or are locked out of your account:

1. Go to the self-service password reset portal at `https://password.company.com`.
2. Enter your employee email address.
3. You will receive an SMS or Microsoft Authenticator notification to verify your identity.
4. Enter the verification code and set a new password.
5. **Requirements**: Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and include numbers, uppercase letters, and symbols.

If you are completely locked out and cannot use the self-service portal, you must contact the IT Helpdesk directly via phone for identity verification.
